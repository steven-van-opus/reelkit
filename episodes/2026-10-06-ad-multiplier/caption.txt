Got an ad that already works? Higgsfield just gave Ad Multiplier a visual interface, and it's built for remaking that ad as your own.

Upload the original, add your product and your cast, and it keeps the shots, the timing and the audio while it swaps in your stuff.

From there, move it somewhere new, try as many hooks as you want without re-uploading, or just type the change. In Higgsfield's demo, that's "have her open the juice while she talks."

To start, open Ad Multiplier in Ads Studio and upload one reference video, 4 to 30 seconds long. You're rebuilding an existing ad, so use one you have the rights to.

Every clip in this reel is one of Higgsfield's own demos.
