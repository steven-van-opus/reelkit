Figma's AI agent just left beta, and it's on every plan.

Hit Cmd/Ctrl + Enter, describe the edit you want, and it makes the change right in your file. In Figma's own example, it swaps a garden scene for a mushroom closeup.

It can now search your other Figma files for context, or you can attach them from the + menu in the prompt box.

On Professional, Organization and Enterprise plans, you can add a guidelines file to a design library, and the agent reads it every time you prompt with that library on.

One heads-up: beta use didn't cost credits, but since Oct 6 the agent uses AI credits, from the same budget as Figma Make.

Source: Figma release notes and Figma Help Center
